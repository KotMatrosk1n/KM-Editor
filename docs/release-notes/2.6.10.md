## Highlights

KM Editor 2.6.10 fixes Restore Missing Pokemon in Sword and Shield, including failures when enabling Mega Beedrill, preparing regional forms, and converting assets for the Mareep and Spoink families.

## Sword And Shield

* Enable Mega Beedrill and other affected Megas in projects with older egg mappings. Apply corrects the form pointer and preserves existing egg moves. Projects where the egg mapping was already corrected work too.
* Restore Hisuian Growlithe, Arcanine, Voltorb, and Electrode, and Alolan Geodude, Graveler, Golem, Grimer, and Muk with their intended hatch forms. Paras and Parasect use compatible catch rates, and Girafarig omits the unavailable Farigiraf evolution.
* Prepare Mareep, Flaaffy, Ampharos, Spoink, and Grumpig with corrected model assets. If you already installed one of these restorations, use Update Asset to replace its assets while keeping your gameplay edits.
* Prepare multiple Legends Arceus restorations in one session without earlier preparations consuming the later ones' archive budget.
* Registered choices appear before they are installed. The Pokemon editor recognizes canonical form identities in expanded personal tables, and restoration staging and saved selections support the full catalog.
