## Highlights

KM Editor 2.6.12 fixes large restoration batches and Mega model conflicts in Sword and Shield, and restores the full TM compatibility list in Scarlet and Violet.

## Sword And Shield

* Stage and apply the full Restore Missing Pokemon catalog in one batch. Large batches have more room for output files and the shared sound bank.
* Keep regular female models and Mega models in separate files. Reenabling an older restoration moves its retained model files and repairs affected egg mappings while keeping compatible customizations and egg moves.

## Scarlet And Violet

* See the full TM compatibility list in the Pokemon editor in every supported language, including TMs a Pokemon cannot currently learn.
