Static party artwork is provided by PokeSprite, maintained by Michiel Sikma and
the project's contributors:
https://github.com/msikma/pokesprite
https://github.com/msikma/pokesprite/blob/master/contributors.md

The PNG files other than the exceptions described below
are unchanged regular sprites from pokemon-gen8 at revision
c5aaa610ff2acdf7fd8e2dccd181bca8be9fcb3e. The original sprite extraction is credited
upstream to Zhorken and Kaphotics. Full contributor credits are linked above.

Annihilape uses the Gen 9 miniature contributed to the PokeSprite collection
by koenigderluegner, at revision 5f0c10b3baa18e1755d586aa1bfd61125df59cc1:
https://github.com/koenigderluegner/pokesprite/blob/5f0c10b3baa18e1755d586aa1bfd61125df59cc1/pokemon-gen8/regular/annihilape.png
https://github.com/koenigderluegner/pokesprite/blob/5f0c10b3baa18e1755d586aa1bfd61125df59cc1/contributors.md
Its 68 by 68 source canvas is adapted to 68 by 56 by removing twelve fully
transparent top rows. All artwork pixels, colors, and horizontal padding are
unchanged.

Origin Dialga and Origin Palkia use the regular PokeSprite artwork at the
revision above, resized with nearest pixel sampling to 29 by 34 and 34 by 32
pixels respectively. The artwork is centered on the 68 by 56 canvas with its
bottom row at 51, preserving binary transparency and the sampled colors.

KM Editor converts the 68 by 56 artwork to the native 136 by 112 party icon
canvas by repeating each source pixel twice on both axes. The source images
retain their original padding except for the Annihilape, Origin, Mega, and Primal canvas adjustments
described above and are not animated.
